- 2026-10-31: Tuning rule: one change per preset, in round steps, made to the threshold furthest from the source's own definition (Piotroski's F ≥ 8 "high score" kept, the quintile cut loosened; the Rule of 40's 40% kept, the size floor raised). Candidates were compared on counts only (scratch script prints totals, no names).
- 2026-10-31: Market-cap floors were tried first as a universe-only lever and rejected: at a $2B floor shareholder-yield still returned 295, margin-inflection 178 and dilution-watch 281, and a floor also moves every universe percentile.
- 2026-10-31: piotroski-value: 426 of the 561 cheapest-quintile companies have no F-score (`piotroski_f` needs all nine inputs, long-term debt tagged). The low count is mostly missing data, not strict thresholds.
- 2026-10-31: dilution-watch is a **deliberate exception** to the 10–100 target (user decision): spec thresholds kept (shares ≥ 10% OR SBC ≥ 15%), sorted by share-count change, 1,030 matches. It is a check to run candidates against, not a list to browse, and how_to_read says so. Tried: no single round threshold reaches ~100 (shares ≥ 100% and SBC ≥ 100% still gives 367); a $300M floor with spec thresholds gives 600 of 2,689 (over the user's 300 cut-off); $2B or $10B floors (81 at 50%/50%, 89 at spec thresholds) rejected because they hide the small caps where dilution matters. The tail is real dilution (share-count change: median +0.7%, p90 +67%, p95 +241%).
