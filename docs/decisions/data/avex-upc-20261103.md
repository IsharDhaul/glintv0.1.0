## The Up-C structure

- The filing says AEVEX Corp. "has a minority economic interest in Holdings LLC". It consolidates Holdings LLC and records the rest as a redeemable noncontrolling interest: `RedeemableNoncontrollingInterestEquityCarryingAmount` was $1,202,666K at 2026-06-30.
- The ownership split is tagged as `MinorityInterestOwnershipPercentageByNoncontrollingOwners`: 0.495 on `SeriesAUnitsMember` and 0.505 on `SeriesBUnitsMember`. This matches the cover counts: 56.47M ÷ 114.04M = 49.5%.
